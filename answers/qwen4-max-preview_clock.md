44 times.
The minute hand moves at 6°/min and the hour hand at 0.5°/min, so the minute hand gains on the hour hand at 5.5°/min. The hands are perpendicular whenever that relative angle is 90° or 270° — i.e., twice per relative revolution, or once every 180/5.5 = 32 8/11 minutes.
In 24 hours (1440 minutes): 1440 ÷ (360/11) = 44.
(Equivalently: the minute hand laps the hour hand 11 times in 12 hours, giving 22 right angles per 12 hours, so 44 per day — not 48, because the "missing" cases near 3 o'clock and 9 o'clock coincide with the neighboring hour's count.)
